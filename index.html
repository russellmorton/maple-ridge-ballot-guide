Maple Ridge Ballot Guide

Two elections, one week apart, in October 2026.

Municipal guide → Saturday, Oct 17 Provincial guide → Saturday, Oct 24

Maple Ridge voted for mayor, council and school trustees on Saturday, October 17, then for its MLAs in the B.C. provincial election on Saturday, October 24. They were separate elections with separate ballots, run by different offices.

An archive of the 2026 campaigns

Both elections are over. Each guide now shows the official results alongside what the candidates said during the campaign. The guides were archived on November 19, 2026 and are no longer updated.
