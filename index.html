Maple Ridge Ballot Guide

Two elections, one week apart.

Maple Ridge votes for mayor, council and school trustees on Saturday, October 17, then for its MLAs in the B.C. provincial election on Saturday, October 24. They're separate elections with separate ballots, run by different offices. Voting in one doesn't count for the other.
